Sites for developers fc-model & fc-bind

Zero-JS Reactivity

Two-Way State Binding Directly in HTML

reactive-view.html HTML Puro 
<!-- Two-Way Binding -->
<input fc-model="userName" 
       placeholder="Tu nombre..." />

<!-- Reactividad en vivo -->
<p>¡Hola, <span fc-bind="userName">invitado</span>!</p>

<!-- Condicional afirmativo e inverso -->
<input type="checkbox" fc-model="activo" />
<div fc-show="activo">Activado 🎉</div>
<div fc-show="!activo">Desactivado 😴</div>

<!-- Click declarativo -->
<button fc-click="Flut.toast('info', '¡Listo!')">
  Toast
</button>
